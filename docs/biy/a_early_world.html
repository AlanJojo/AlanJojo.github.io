Introduction to the Early World

Conversation b/w Fr. Mike Schmitz and Jeff Cavins (Great Adventure Bible Timeline)

Bible Translation: Revised Standard Version - Catholic Edition (RSV-CE)

  • Jeff Cavins
    • Great Adventure Bible Timeline - 12 periods
    • Out of the 73 books, not all are equal. All are inspired; but they don't all do the same thing.
    • Some books are narrative, some are prophetic, some are poetry, some are apocalyptic.
    • We pick 14 narrative books and the other 59 books are read in the historical context along with these 14 narrative books.
    • The Early World consists of the first 11 chapters of Genesis (till Jan 5th).
      • Here, we see themes/patterns that are going to be revisited later in the Bible.
      • It is a history of the early world, and it is written in Hebrew poetry form (not just a literal way).
      • It make it less historical. It's just the way God had it written.
      • When we get up to the Patriarchs, we will see that it’s more of a linear type of history.
  • Fr. Mike
    • Chapter 01-11 : Pre-History
    • Chapter 12-50 : (Mode switches) History
  • Jeff Cavins
    • Creation deals with the formlessness, and then God fills the void
      • First 3 days of creation - God creates time, space and land
      • Days 4,5 and 6 - God fills the space
    • Creation of world -> Creation of Adam and Eve
      • Human is created in a different way - In the image and likeness of God, have will, capacity to love and sacrifice
      • Caveats: Free to eat of any tree other than Tree of Knowledge and of Good and Evil.
      • Chapter 3: Enemy comes in, immediately starts questioning God; and lies.
    • Heart of Sin: Pride came in. Lost their trust. Were grasping for the creation rather than The Creator.
      • Sin changed the relationship with each other, with the creations and with The Creator.
    • Good news: God has a plan of sheer goodness.
      • The remedy for the brokenness is extremely radical.
      • God becomes one of us. God takes our place and pays the price out of love for us.
    • As we see the rest of the Early world play out, we see the results of sin of Adam and Eve.
    • While reading, will see the plan, the problems arising, and the remedy.
  • Fr. Mike
    • Attitude of many characters in the Bible - I know better that God
    • And we see the effect of their actions.
