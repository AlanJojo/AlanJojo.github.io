Day 001 - In the Beginning

Translation used - New Revised Standard Version 2nd Catholic Edition (NRSV-CE)

Early World Period

Genesis 1 - 2

Psalm 19

Based on - The Bible in a Year podcast (Fr. Mike Schmitz, Ascension Presents)

  • Trajectory of the entire Bible is based off of the uniqueness of Genesis 1 and 2
    • In any of the other ancient genesis stories, the creation is born out of violence, of sexual domination, or of sexual act.
    • "In the beginning, God created [Hebrew: bara]". The word "bara" means 'creation out of nothing'. It is found in no other genesis story.
    • Other genesis stories tells that their gods created man to be slaves, for sport, or to toy with them for entertainment. Work is beneath these gods.
      • In Genesis, we see God working. He is the Creator.
    • The Catechism says: "God, infinitely perfect and blessed in Himself, in a plan of sheer goodness, freely created man to make him share in His own blessed life."
    • In no other world view other than the Judeo-Christian world view, is man created in the image and likeness of God. This is where the idea that human beings have dignity comes from.
      • Humans don't get their dignity from their strength, power, beauty, wisdom or government.
      • We get it from the fact that every human being is made in God's image and likeness to share in His own blessed life.

  • Gen 2:15 - He made man for at-least for 3 purposes: for labor, for leisure and for love.
  • We saw God working. He tells man to go to work. i.e. God tells: part of what makes you like me is that you can labor like me.

  • Genesis 1 is macro-event(poetic). Genesis 2 is the micro-event(zoomed in version). They are both same accounts of different events.
    • Day 1 - Separation b/w Light, Darkness
    • Day 2 - Separation b/w Water and Sky
    • Day 3 - Separation b/w Water and Land
    • Day 4 - Sun, Moon, Stars (what rules light/darkness)
    • Day 5 - Things in Sea, Sky (what rules water and sky)
    • Day 6 - Beasts, Human (what rules land)

  • Genesis 2 - This account of the state of the world at the beginning, which introduces the story of the first sin, comes from a different and earlier source and is composed in a very different style.
  • There is nothing in these chapters that commits us to any particular scientific view of the origins of the world or man, or that would exclude the evolution hypothesis.

  • Gen 2:20 - Just because the word helper [Hebrew: Ezer Kenegdo] is used, it doesn't mean that the woman is lower (just a helpmate). It means something more profound.
    • In Old testament, the word 'Ezer' is used 21 times, out of which 19 is in reference to God. (That God is my helper)
    • When God took Eve from Adam, He did not take her from his head to lord it over him, nor from his foot to be walked upon by him. He took her from his side to walk with him, from beneath his arm to be guarded by him, and from near his heart to be loved by him.
  • Gen 2:23 - [Hebrew: Man] = "ish"; [Hebrew: Woman] = "ishshah"

  • When God made this world, he made it absolutely good.
    • We will find out what happened to this world. We live in a good world that has been broken.

Based on - Haydock's Catholic Bible Commentary (1859)

  • The Book of Genesis
    • Creation of all things out of nothing.
    • Refutation of all the heathenish mythology, and of the world's eternity (argued by Aristotle)
    • We behold the short reign of innocence, and the origin of sin and misery, the dispersion of nations, and the providence of God watching over his chosen people, till the death of Joseph (B.C. 1631). 
    • The first word in this book is [Hebrew: Bereshith] meaning `Genesis`. This is also the first word in 'The Gospel of John'.
