Day 002 - The Fall of Adam and Eve

Early World Period

Genesis 03 - 04

Psalm 104

  • Psalm 104 is a magnificient hymn of creation

  • Genesis 3: Temptation of Eve
    • The term used to describe satan is the term which, in other parts of the Bible means 'Leviathan'(sea monster/dragon) - fearsome/powerful creature.
    • He doesn't overtly(openly) challenge God's existence or God's authority to declare off-limits but he does challenge God's trustworthiness.
    • Will you belong to Him or take your life in your own hands? This is the test for each one of us.
  • God doesn't simply want us to believe in Him. The point is to belong to Him.
  • Even if God reveals Himself, His goodness, His power, His beauty and His existence, we would still have to go through the same exact challenge that Eve and Adam had gone through.
    • The question is not `Do you believe in God?`
    • The question is `Do you belong to Him?`, `Will you obey Him because you know that He loves you?`.
  • First challenge of serpent - God doesn't love you. Else, He would have allowed you to eat from that tree.
  • God comes along and calls out.
    • After Adam tells God that he is naked, the next line: Who told you that you were naked? Have you eaten of the tree of which I commanded you not to eat?
    • How we hear it tells us what we need to know about our vision of God
      • We could hear it as as God's anger and wrath.
      • Or we could hear the true Heart of the Father. Do we hear the Heartbreak of the Father in His voice. Because He sees the great plans He had for them ruined.

  • Gen 3:15 - He shall bruise your head. i.e. the seed of the woman(mankind descended from Eve), will eventually gain the victory over the powers of evil.
  • Only the serpent gets cursed. Humans get remedies (painful ones; because they failed to choose Love). Humans were created to love. Love always involves sacrifice.
    • For Eve to love - bring forth life in pain and learn that Love involves sacrifice.
    • For Adam to care for his wife and care for his family - he would have to work among thrones and thistles.
  • How do we know that this is not God's wrath?
    • After he drives Adam and Eve from Eden, he puts the Cherubium to guard the tree of life.
    • If they eat of the tree of life, they will never die. God doesn't want them to live in this brokenness forever.
    • Maybe their destiny was to live forever in wholeness and holiness. But since they are broken, God is saying that you will end up dying so that you can be raised up.
    • How do we know that God is still caring?
      • Gen 3:21 - Lord made for Adam and Eve garments of skins, and clothed them.
        God continues to provide.
      • "Garments of skins" - something had to die
      • Connection b/w Love and sacrifice.
  • The name Eve[Hebrew: Chavah/Havah] resembles the word for living (chavah -> to breathe ; chayah -> to live/to give life).

  • Genesis 4: Things escalate. Humanity goes from eating a fruit to fratricide(killing own brother)
  • Why did God accepted Abel's offering but disregarded Cain's? Ultimately we don't know.
    • One of the thoughts: The nature of Abel's offering was that he had brought of the firstlings of his flock.
    • Nature of Cain's offering was that he just gave God whatever (not necessarily first fruits).
    • Do I give God the best or do I just give whatever is left? One offering gives glory to God the other might in some ways meaningless.
      • Not because it's meaningless to God per se, but because it was meaningless to us.
      • We just gave God whatever was left.
    • First pebble to go to my daily calendar - time for prayer. And make sure that it doesn't get moved.

  • Cain was angry with his brother. God says: If you do well will you be not be accepted. But if you don't, sin is lurking at the door. It's desire is for you. But you must master it.
    • Even when we are broken, even when we are angry, even when we are tempted, God is declaring that we do not have to sin.
    • You can be the master of the evil that's within you.

  • Gen 4:16 - Cain dwelt in the land of Nod(Hebrew: to wander)
  • Gen 4:26 - Seth is the ancestor of Noah.

  • Love involves sacrifice. In order to love, I have to sacrifice something. Each day, I have to sacrifice some of my time to spend with God(to be in prayer). I want that time to be not whatever is left, but the best.
